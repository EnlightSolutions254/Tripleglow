About Tripleglow Housekeeping Services

Kenya's dedicated cleaning team, headquartered in Nakuru — built on trained people and proven process.

Our Story

Built on Trust, One Clean Space at a Time

Tripleglow Housekeeping Services was founded in Nakuru to give homes and businesses a cleaning partner they could rely on without question. What started as a small housekeeping outfit has grown into a full-service cleaning company trusted by families, landlords, offices and commercial sites across Kenya, with our headquarters still based in Nakuru.

Every member of our team is professionally trained, and every job follows the same well-established Standard Operating Procedures — so the quality you get on your first clean is the same quality you get on your fiftieth.

We use high-quality, approved detergents and materials, work flexibly around each client's schedule, and measure our success by one thing: outstanding customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To deliver the best cleaning services across Kenya through trained staff, approved materials and dependable Standard Operating Procedures — giving every client a hygienic space and genuine peace of mind.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted housekeeping and hygiene company in Kenya — known for professionalism, reliability and consistently excellent results across every home and business we serve.
